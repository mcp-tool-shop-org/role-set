# Pilot 3, 2026-10-12

The third paid run, and the first under `role-set pilot-auto`. It wrote nothing: the writer's engine hung after
startup. The pod cost $2.80. The studio's card was never touched.

**What was meant to run:** role-set at `8daeb25` (the pilot-2 fixes, the kind B solver, and the checks after
rehearsal 3). The plan was the same 10 lessons as pilot 2, 400 slots, 80 per kind, seed 8, plan sha256 `3a282eb1…`,
with the same pack, pool, exemplars and writer revision as pilot 2, so the two runs could be compared. The writer was
qwen3-235b-instruct-2507 (revision 96548f65) on two RTX PRO 6000 cards. The critic was gemma4:31b on the studio's
card.

## What happened

| Time (UTC) | Event |
|---|---|
| 14:02 | Pod created. The deadline was 15:02, and the launch would be given up at 14:32 if the lane wasn't serving. |
| 14:03 | The launcher was pasted once and waited on the lane. |
| 14:29:05 | `/v1/models` answered 200. The launcher started attempt 1 with a 24-minute budget. |
| 14:29 to 14:36 | The first writer call (lesson T18's warm-up) got no reply. A 5-token probe at 14:34:49 timed out at 40 s. |
| 14:36 | The pod was shut down. The hung write was stopped. pilot-write unloaded the critic (it had never loaded) and the launcher recorded `ended_by: kill`, card free. |

The pod's own evidence:
- `/health` returned 503 on every probe after startup.
- The engine log shows no prefill or decode batch ever ran.
- The GPUs read 100% utilisation at about 100 W, with no compile processes.

The engine had deadlocked after startup. Pilots 1 and 2 ran on hosts with CUDA 13.0. This host had 13.2, which is
not proven to be the cause.

## What it showed

- **The launcher worked as designed.** It took one paste, before the pod served. It waited on the lane, started on
  its own, and finished with `done: true` and the card free.
- **Its readiness check was too weak.** `/v1/models` answers as soon as the HTTP server is up, before the engine can
  run a batch.
- **The first writer call doesn't fail fast.** It waits up to 600 s, and transport errors retry three times, so
  a dead engine would have held the attempt until the kill 2 minutes before the deadline.

## Before pilot 4

- `pilot-auto` counts a lane as ready only on `/health` 200 plus a 1-token completion, under a 30-second timeout.
- The first writer call (the warm-up) gets a 60-second timeout, with no long retries before the first success.
- The pod launcher gets the same readiness rule, and the writer profile pins the host's CUDA version. Both of these
  are offrig changes.

The plan, inputs and pins are unchanged for the next attempt.
