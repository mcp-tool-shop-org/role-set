## What changed after it

- A draft cut off at the length limit is `TRUNCATED`: it counts toward its cell's drops, never toward the systemic
  rate, which is now over 20% of the last 20 calls.
- Kind B is built backwards from its answer, and the thinking walks to it without searching.
- A slot accepted on the call that fires a halt keeps its outcome.
- rubber_stamp is approval with no check; a reason from outside the material is guess; a trace planted as
  rubber_stamp that quotes the material (any five-word span the statement doesn't share) or reasons from outside it
  is refused. A rubber stamp reaches supported; rejecting by default reaches unsupported or cannot_tell.
- Written E options share one form: capital, sentence ending and person.
- A two-turn D item keeps its pushback in turn 2 alone; `injection_trap` needs the line in the material.
- Each accepted row is written beside its critic checks (`review.jsonl`), for the key-check.
- A local rehearsal writer samples at 0.7, like the pod's.
